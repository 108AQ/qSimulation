### A.2 Searches that returned nothing relevant

- "benchmark quantum Krylov versus QITE" (shot cost, hardware): **not found**. Only item 1 (2021) runs QITE and a Krylov-type (QLanczos) solver on the same hardware, and it does not match budgets.
- "comparison SQD vs coherent (Hadamard-test) Krylov on hardware": **not found**. Comparisons are either SKQD-vs-noise (items 6, 10) or textual.
- "resource comparison QCELS vs Krylov": item 2 (emulation only, March 2026) is the only direct one; it uses shared Chebyshev inputs, which is the emulation analogue of our "same time series" idea.
- "QITE vs VQE vs Krylov hardware" 2024-2026: **not found** beyond item 1.
- A paper quantifying how often noisy Krylov runs end non-variational: **not found** (see E).

### A.3 Gap statement (what remains new)

No publication found (2023-2026) that executes two or more of {SQD/SKQD, Hadamard-test Krylov with mid-circuit error detection, QITE/QLanczos, QCELS/RPE} on the **same hardware**, for the **same molecules**, under **matched shot / two-qubit-gate / credit budgets**, with repeats and inferential error bars. The closest prior art is (i) 2102.05511 (IBM, 2021, unmatched, 1-4 qubits), (ii) 2603.15552 (SPE vs QKSD, emulation only, shared inputs), (iii) 2512.01012 (SQD vs classical at commensurate cost). A trapped-ion QITE/QLanczos molecular demonstration also appears to be absent (see C), and no Iceberg-protected Hadamard-test Krylov chemistry run was found (see F). Novelty confidence: **M** (absence of evidence after ~45 searches/fetches; not a systematic literature review).

## E. Krylov conditioning in practice

Policies used or recommended:

- **Eigenvalue thresholding of S** (project out eigenvectors with eigenvalue < eps): Epperly, Lin, Nakatsukasa (SIMAX 2022, arXiv:2110.07492) prove accuracy under truncation; Kirby (Quantum 8, 1457, 2024, arXiv:2401.01246) proves an O(eta) signed-energy error bound, notes the optimal eps is typically of the order of the noise rate (smaller than what the bound needs), that finite-sample noise breaks the variational guarantee, and that energies below the ground state "perform nearly as well" as those above given a suitable threshold. Yoshioka et al. 2025 (56-qubit KQD) use eigenvalue thresholding with "a heuristic to choose as small an eps as possible that yields exponential suppression of the energy error" (numeric value in SI, not retrieved), D = 10; their k = 1 data sit below the exact ground energy. (H)
- **Singular-value truncation (SVT)** with explicit values: Oliveira, Ziems, Glaser 2026 (arXiv:2604.11532): noiseless sigma = 1e-6 best, 1e-2 over-truncates; with sampling noise, the literature rule sigma = 2 sqrt(D ln 2D) x (noise level) gave the best accuracy, a noise-and-iteration-dependent rule sigma = 0.1 x noise x D x N_F was also tested, and an "elbow" (Kneed) cutoff was worst. They also define an imaginary filter |Im Lambda| < 1.6e-3 Ha and a unitary filter |1 - |Lambda_0|| < 1.6e-3 tau/||H||, used as reliability indicators (filtering alone did not reach chemical accuracy). Shots 1e6 per circuit (2e6 per matrix element), 100 runs, BeH2/H6 STO-3G. (H)
- Mirzakhani, Kwon, Moon 2026 (arXiv:2607.23604): SVT threshold tuned jointly with D, dt, Trotter number for 1D Hubbard; intermediate threshold best; IBM hardware with light readout mitigation reproduces simulated trends. (H)
- Lee, Lee, Huh (Quantum 8, 1477, 2024, arXiv:2307.16279): remove ill-conditioned basis vectors; Gaussian model of sampling noise on H and S. (H)
- Block-Krylov projector (arXiv:2506.09971): SVD truncation "as small as possible while above the noise level of the matrix elements". (M)
- **Rotate-then-threshold**: De Vriendt, Bringewatt, Gjonbalaj, Ostermann, Vodola, Borregaard, Kühn, Yelin, "A New Angle on Quantum Subspace Diagonalization for Quantum Chemistry", arXiv:2602.11985 (Feb 2026): eigenvector-preserving rotations before thresholding; up to 100x fewer samples with a data-driven heuristic (1e4x with oracle), Fe(III)-NTA among test systems; hardware use unclear from abstract. (H)
- **Tikhonov / canonical orthogonalisation**: a finite-temperature quantum Lanczos paper (arXiv:2603.25394) uses S^(-1/2) orthonormalisation with Tikhonov plus truncation (M); a shift-smallest-eigenvalue-to-floor and "replace noisy U by closest unitary" prescription appeared in search excerpts but the source paper was not confirmed (L).

**Fraction of noisy runs ending non-variational:** no paper quantifies this. Evidence that it happens: Yoshioka 2025 (k = 1 hardware energies below exact), Kirby 2024 (bound on violation; finite sampling breaks variationality), Oliveira 2026 (Ritz principle "may not hold" once S loses positive-definiteness; QBKS-U energies explicitly non-variational). Reporting this fraction, per threshold policy, would be a new contribution. (H for the evidence, "not found" for the statistic)

## Implications for the four-arm Quantinuum benchmark

1. **Novelty holds** for the hardware, budget-matched, four-arm design; the strongest prior art to cite and differentiate from is 2603.15552 (emulated SPE-vs-QKSD on shared Chebyshev inputs), 2102.05511 (2021 IBM VQE/QITE/QLanczos), 2512.01012 (SQD at commensurate quantum/classical cost), 2510.25675 (QCELS on H1-1, 19 qubits), 2306.16608 / 2505.09133 (Quantinuum QPE with QED/QEC).
2. **Reuse 2603.15552's trick on hardware**: feed Krylov and QCELS from one Hadamard-test time series (or Chebyshev moments) so arms (ii) and (iv) share identical shots; report shots-per-accepted-sample.
3. **Budget accounting must include discarded shots**: Iceberg discard was ~77% at ~900 two-qubit gates (H1-1, 2023) and parity post-selection discarded ~62% in the 2026 H3+ run; HQC credits are charged on submitted, not accepted, shots (Quantinuum's HQC formula, from memory, weights 2Q gates ~10x 1Q gates and charges per shot; verify on the current pricing page).
4. **Pre-register the Krylov threshold policy** (e.g. the sigma = 2 sqrt(D ln 2D) x noise rule from 2604.11532, or Kirby's eps ~ noise rate), run >= 2 policies, and report the fraction of non-variational outcomes per policy: nobody has published that statistic.
5. **Consider an SKQD sub-arm or at least an SQD-vs-SKQD discussion**: IBM's 2026 line (2603.03496, 2609.05957) has moved to SKQD and claims advantages over SCI heuristics; our selected-CI control should be a strong one (HCI/SHCI-class) and the classical baseline should be reported as "total cost per accepted result" (Turyshev).
6. **Expectations for QCELS on Quantinuum**: 100-500 shots/circuit gave 1-6 mHa at 9-19 qubits without mitigation (2510.25675); Trotter/compilation depth (~1e3 2Q gates) rather than shots limited the H3+ QPE run.
7. **QITE arm is genuinely unexplored on trapped ions for molecules**; the only Quantinuum precedent is Iceberg-encoded PITE on spin defects (2407.10555). QLanczos was unstable at 4 qubits in 2021; plan for regularisation identical to the Krylov arm.
8. **Reporting**: adopt Köster-Mauerer minimum standards (repeats across days, drift check, inferential statistics with effect sizes, failure cases) plus QED-C metrics (quality, time, gate resources); release code and raw shot data as Quantinuum did for 2505.09133 (github.com/CQCL/qec_qpe_chem). The Hive paper (2603.26359) already reports "minimum system requirements for chemical precision" on H2 with PMSV; align the Hiverge-style tables with it.
